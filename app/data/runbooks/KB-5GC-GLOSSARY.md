# Reference interfaces

- N2: control plane between gNB and AMF over NGAP/SCTP.
- N3: user plane between gNB and UPF over GTP-U.
- N4: PFCP between SMF and UPF for session rules.
- N8: AMF to UDM. N11: AMF to SMF.
- S1-MME: eNB to MME (S1AP/SCTP). S6a: MME to HSS via DRA (Diameter). S11: MME to SGW (GTP-C).
- Cx: I-CSCF/S-CSCF to HSS (Diameter). Mw: between CSCFs (SIP). Gm: UE to P-CSCF (SIP).
- KPIs: registration success rate (AMF), PDU session success rate (SMF), attach success rate (MME), IMS registration success and VoLTE call setup success (CSCF).
